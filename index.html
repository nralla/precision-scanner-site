3D scans scaled by a caliper

Precision Scanner makes 3D scans of small objects and rooms with a LiDAR-equipped iPhone Pro, then scales the model to a real length you measured with a caliper. Free, with no account and no data collected.

Scanning

  • Walk around the object; the ring on screen shows which angles have been captured.
  • After the first orbit, turn the object over and capture a second orbit so the underside is included.
  • Or keep the phone still and turn the object on a turntable; blurred shots are rejected.
  • The model is built on the phone, with no internet connection.

Caliper calibration

  • Pick two opposite faces on the model and enter your caliper reading; the model is scaled to it.
  • The angle between the faces is shown, and step heights can be measured too.
  • Check a second dimension against your calipers and see the difference in millimetres.

Export

  • Textured OBJ, STL and PLY in millimetres, plus USDZ.
  • Export the source photos as a ZIP to reprocess them in desktop photogrammetry software.
  • A project report that summarises measurements and files.

First print

  • Share printable caliper, stepped block and ring files from the app and print them.

Room scanning

  • Walls, doors, windows and furniture with RoomPlan.

Requirements

  • Object and room scanning need a LiDAR-equipped iPhone Pro and iOS 18 or later.
  • On iPhones with Face ID but no LiDAR, only the front-camera TrueDepth scan is available.
Good to know. The model built on the phone is a quick preview. Its scale comes from the caliper value you enter; without calibration, dimensions can be off by several percent. Shiny, metallic and transparent objects scan poorly.
