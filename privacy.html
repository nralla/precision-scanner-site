Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Precision Scanner uses the camera to make 3D scans of objects and rooms on an iPhone or iPad. This policy explains which data the app handles and where that data stays.

Data collected

The app sends no data to the developer or to anyone else. There is no account, analytics, advertising or tracking, and the app's own code makes no internet connections.

What is stored on your device

  • Photos taken during a scan and, on devices with LiDAR, the LiDAR depth data recorded with them
  • 3D models of objects and rooms, and point clouds from TrueDepth scans
  • Measurements and calibration values you enter

These are stored only in the app's folder on your device. If iCloud Backup or computer backup is on, Apple's backup system may include that folder; the developer has no access to those backups.

Camera, TrueDepth and face data

The camera is used only when you start a scan. You can turn off camera access at any time in Settings.

The TrueDepth scan in Quick Scan uses the front camera's TrueDepth sensor as a depth sensor. Before every TrueDepth scan the app shows what the scan collects, and the sensor is used only after you tap "I Agree, Start Scan".

  • What is collected: depth measurements of whatever is in front of the front camera. If you point it at a face, the scan contains the 3D shape of that face. The app does not use face tracking, face meshes, facial landmarks or expressions, does not recognise or identify faces, and saves no camera images in this mode.
  • How it is used: each depth frame is turned into 3D points on the device and then discarded. Only the resulting point cloud (point positions, no colour) and its measured size are kept, to show you the scan and its dimensions. Face data is never used for advertising, marketing, authentication, profiling or any other purpose.
  • Where it is stored: only in the app's folder on your device, as described above. The app never sends it off the device.
  • Sharing: the app never shares face data with the developer or with any third party, including advertising and analytics providers. Apart from your own device backups described above, a scan leaves the device only if you export it yourself (see Sharing).
  • Retention and deletion: the point cloud stays on your device until you delete the scan in the Library or delete the app.

Sharing

When you export a model, photos or a report, the file goes to the app or person you choose in the share sheet. You start that transfer; the app never sends anything by itself.

Deleting data

You can delete scans in the Library. Deleting the app also deletes all of its data on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text ships with an app update.
