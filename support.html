Support

Questions, problems and suggestions are welcome.

Contact. Open an issue on GitHub. A free GitHub account is needed. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

For a good scan

  1. Put the object on a rigid, matte surface. Not a towel or a blanket: the object sinks in and the fabric merges into the model.
  2. Keep direct sunlight off the object. Close the curtain or move to even indoor light; glare turns into fake bumps on smooth surfaces.
  3. Walk slowly around the object until the ring on screen is full. Keep the whole object in the frame.
  4. When the orbit is done, tap Pause and Flip the Object, turn it over and capture a second orbit. Without it, the underside is missing.
  5. If the second orbit will not close, tap Stop This Orbit, Build Model. Your photos are kept.

After scanning

  • Scale: Library → model → Calibrate with Caliper → tap two opposite faces → enter the length you measured.
  • Check: Check a second dimension shows the difference between the model and your caliper in millimetres.
  • Interrupted scan: if the app closed during a scan, open Library → Unfinished scans to package the photos (.zip) or delete them.

Devices and privacy

  • Object scanning needs a LiDAR-equipped iPhone Pro and iOS 18 or later.
  • The app collects no data; your scans stay on your phone. See the <a href="privacy.html">privacy policy</a>.
